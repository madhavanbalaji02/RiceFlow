Limitations

Thresholds are configurable choices, not validated biological cutoffs. Single-accession regional VCF observations are not whole-genome measurements. Reference calls and unsupported variants are counted outside the SNP metric denominator. Not-assessed qualities are masked conservatively and reported separately from failures. Source FILTER flags are preserved, not reinterpreted. Already pruned CoreSNP data cannot reproduce raw discovery, LD structure, or original DP/GQ filtering. No imputation, pangenome analysis, or population discovery is claimed.
